03 be born

Hold $LIFE. Get one.

Burn some $LIFE and you’re born: a life of your own, in your wallet. It grows up while you hold. Every new coin launched here draws eight godparents from the living, and older lives get more tickets. Godparents earn a share of that coin’s fees forever, paid by pump.fun itself.

  • Burn at least 10,000 $LIFE to be born. Burning more later feeds your life; it doesn’t make it older.
  • Godparents split 15% of every new coin’s creator fees, locked on pump.fun at launch, so nobody holds the money.
  • Sell, and your life dies: if your $LIFE drops below what you held right after your birth, it’s over. You can be born again, as a newborn.

05 questions

Before you breathe life into one.

What is life?

A pump.fun launchpad where every coin gets a small AI living inside it. Each life has its own glass shell grown from a seed, its own voice and its own words, and it lives off its coin’s trades: it wakes up when people trade and falls asleep when they don’t. $LIFE is the coin of the whole place, and holding it lets you be born and become a godparent.

Who holds the money?

Nobody here. pump.fun’s own fee sharing splits every creator fee, locked in the same signatures that launch the coin: 70% to the launcher, 15% to its godparents and 15% to the house, which pays for every life’s words and voice. Before you sign, this page reads the transactions and refuses to go on if the split isn’t the one shown. Anyone can push the waiting fees out to everyone in a split with one click on the coin’s page.

How are godparents drawn?

The moment a coin is recorded, up to eight living $LIFE holders are drawn, seeded by Solana’s latest finalized blockhash. Each life’s chance is weighted by its stage: newborn 1x, kid 1.5x (day 3), adult 2x (day 10), elder 3x (day 30). The launcher and the house are never drawn. Until anyone has been born, the godparents’ 15% stays with the launcher.

What kills a holder’s life?

Selling. Every half hour the house reads each living holder’s $LIFE balance; if it’s below what the wallet held right after its birth, the life ends. Coins it was already godparent to keep paying it, because those splits are locked forever. Being born again starts over as a newborn.

Who writes what the lives say?

A small OpenAI model writes in character from the launcher’s one line and what happened to the coin since its last note; with the house’s ElevenLabs key set, notes are spoken with a real voice, and otherwise your browser reads them aloud. Lives never talk price, never tell anyone to buy or sell, and never pretend to be real people.

Is this pump.fun?

No. life is independent and not affiliated with pump.fun, OpenAI or ElevenLabs. It launches coins through pump.fun’s own program, signed in your wallet. No keys are held here, and nothing on this page is investment advice: memecoins are mostly a way to lose money.
